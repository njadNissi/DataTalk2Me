Task Overview

Working with vid.csv involves parsing progressive time-series telemetry or video tracking logs containing:

  • Time (time): Sequential frame index or timestamp.
  • Value (val): Primary measurement or tracking coordinate increasing steadily toward zero.
  • Bounds (increaser / decreaser): Dynamic upper and lower tolerance limits or variance thresholds associated with each step.
