imu_data.csv

The task is to process multi-axis inertial sensor telemetry tracking acceleration, angular velocity, and orientation.

Task Overview

Working with imu_data.csv involves parsing time-series sensor logs from an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU):

  • Accelerometer (accX, accY, accZ): Measures linear acceleration and gravitational forces across three spatial axes ($g$).
  • Gyroscope (gyroX, gyroY, gyroZ): Measures rotational velocity and rate of change of orientation ($\text{deg/s}$).
  • Orientation (angleX): Computed tilt or rotation angle along the primary axis.
